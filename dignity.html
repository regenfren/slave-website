    Right to Dignity

    The association develops concrete actions and sustainable projects to guarantee the dignity of individuals, especially those in vulnerable situations.

    Goals

    Our Objectives

    01

    Stand with the vulnerable

    Concrete support for elders, children, families and women facing hardship.

    02

    Access to rights

    Guide newcomers through administrative, legal and medical steps — with translation and interpretation.

    03

    Community bonds

    Link generations through workshops, shared activities and a community library.

    04

    Care with limits

    Help with respect for privacy and dignity, never replacing qualified professionals.

    Scope of Our Intervention

    Our actions rely on the commitment of volunteers and take place within a punctual and time-limited framework.

    We support individuals in their procedures (translation, guidance) and help them in the design and development of their projects.

    The association operates within the limits of its skills and resources, without substituting for qualified professionals (legal, medical, or social).

    We also ensure strict respect for the limits of our intervention, as well as the privacy and autonomy of the people we support.
