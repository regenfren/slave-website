Right to Dignity

The association runs practical actions and long-term projects to support individuals and families, strengthen community ties and promote everyone's dignity.

Our Objectives

  1. 01

    There for everyone

    Solidarity and support for older people, children and families.

  2. 02

    Information and guidance

    Help newcomers settle in, with information, translation and interpreting.

  3. 03

    Community bonds

    Link generations through workshops, shared activities and a community library.

  4. 04

    Care with limits

    Help with respect for privacy and dignity, never replacing qualified professionals.

Our Areas of Action

Regular, one-off and on-the-ground help for people in need

Support for the Elderly

  • Help with moving within the care facility
  • Light furniture repairs in rooms
  • Regular visits to combat isolation

Alongside families

Growing together and opening up new possibilities.

  • Building bonds and sharing experiences
  • Support for family projects and initiatives

Community Library

  • Books and resources to find information, learn and get inspired
  • Films and documentaries to discover and borrow
  • A space for exchange and ongoing learning

Furniture Restoration

Light repairs and restoration of furniture for elderly residents: a practical way of caring for people and their dignity.

Ongoing Projects

Intergenerational Support

Bringing young volunteers and elderly people together through shared activities.

  • Cultural workshops
  • Shared activities and outings
  • Light home assistance (repairs, sorting, reading)

Career Guidance & Integration

Building a tailored career path with each person.

  • Adapted career paths (e.g., PE teacher turned tiler, foreign diploma validation via ENIC-NARIC)
  • CV and cover letter writing workshops
  • Connection with training centers (AFPA, CNAM) and integration structures
  • Registration support with France Travail

Scope of Our Intervention

Our work is done by volunteers, on an occasional and time-limited basis.

We help people with their paperwork (translation, guidance) and with planning and developing their own projects.

We work within the limits of our skills and resources and never replace qualified professionals (legal, medical or social).

We keep strictly to these limits and respect the privacy and independence of the people we support.
