Right to Dignity

The association develops concrete actions and sustainable projects to guarantee the dignity of individuals, especially those in vulnerable situations.

Our Objectives

  1. 01

    Stand with the vulnerable

    Concrete support for elders, children, families and women facing hardship.

  2. 02

    Access to rights

    Guide newcomers through administrative, legal and medical steps — with translation and interpretation.

  3. 03

    Community bonds

    Link generations through workshops, shared activities and a community library.

  4. 04

    Care with limits

    Help with respect for privacy and dignity, never replacing qualified professionals.

Our Areas of Action

Recurring, one-time, and field actions to support those in need

Support for the Elderly

  • Help with moving within the care facility
  • Light furniture repairs in rooms
  • Regular visits to combat isolation

Alongside families

Growing together, opening new opportunities.

  • Building bonds and sharing experiences
  • Support for family projects and initiatives

Community Library

  • Books on life in France, social rights, health, and more
  • Lending of films and documentaries
  • A space for exchange and continuous learning

Furniture Restoration

Light repairs and restoration of furniture for elderly residents — a concrete example of our commitment to dignity and care.

Ongoing Projects

Intergenerational Support

Developing cooperation between young volunteers and elderly people through shared activities.

  • Cultural workshops
  • Shared activities and outings
  • Light home assistance (repairs, sorting, reading)

Career Guidance & Integration

Building tailored career paths for people in professional transition.

  • Adapted career paths (e.g., PE teacher turned tiler, foreign diploma validation via ENIC-NARIC)
  • CV and cover letter writing workshops
  • Connection with training centers (AFPA, CNAM) and integration structures
  • Registration support with France Travail

Scope of Our Intervention

Our actions rely on the commitment of volunteers and take place within a punctual and time-limited framework.

We support individuals in their procedures (translation, guidance) and help them in the design and development of their projects.

The association operates within the limits of its skills and resources, without substituting for qualified professionals (legal, medical, or social).

We also ensure strict respect for the limits of our intervention, as well as the privacy and autonomy of the people we support.
